Each photo links to its original product page. Product and brand names may be trademarks.

The applicable derivative database is available at /catalogue/italy.json.gz,
including retained original labels, provenance, retailer relations and derived
policy assessments. It is offered under ODbL 1.0. Share-alike and attribution
obligations apply to redistribution and public use of a derivative database.
This download is explicit; it is never fetched on application startup.

Community records are not independently verified packaging. Ingredient labels
remain research evidence until exact-barcode packaging review is recorded.
Retailer associations describe source contributor tags, not guaranteed current
stock or retailer endorsement. No retailer website content is redistributed.
Halal ingredient assessment is separate from independent certification.
