How HalalCheck works

01

Scan

We use your device camera to read a product barcode. Nothing is uploaded while scanning.

02

Verify

Results are only marked certified when a trusted certification source can be verified. Unknown products are never presented as halal.

03

Understand

Ingredient concerns are explained clearly so you can make your own informed decision.

Important

HalalCheck is an information aid, not a religious authority. Always check the latest packaging and certification mark.
